# SPANEX — copy voice

## Brand descriptor — "Engineering" (owner decision 2026-07-24)
The descriptor is **"SPANEX Engineering"** (wordmark, eyebrow, titles, meta, OG).
The earlier ban on the word "Engineering" was lifted by explicit owner decision.

- "Engineering" is allowed in the descriptor and headings.
- CAUTION (not legal advice): Ontario's Professional Engineers Act restricts
  presenting an engineering practice without proper licensure — confirm
  positioning with counsel for Ontario-facing material.
- Body copy may still use "design / technical production / drafting / analysis"
  where it reads naturally.
- Refer to the client's sealing authority as "your licensed professional".

## Voice
Short declarative sentences. Technical nouns. Specific numbers. Never a
superlative, never an adjective where a figure would do. Contractions are fine;
exclamation marks are not. No emoji. No lorem ipsum.

Every claim must be checkable: "48-hour turnaround" not "fast"; "checked twice"
not "high quality".

## Forbidden marketing words
solutions, synergy, leverage, innovative, cutting-edge, world-class, seamless,
empower, best-in-class, revolutionary. Also avoid cost/savings/rates language and
any "offshore advantage" framing.

## Domain vocabulary (use correctly)
make-ready, load case, framing, guying, anchor lead, capacity, transmittal,
as-built, dead-end pole, tangent pole, SPIDAcalc, joint-use, landbase, clearance,
mid-span sag, pass/fail threshold, CSA C22.3, distributor standard.
